## Count method

I downloaded the official Springer publisher PDF (23 pages) and extracted its text with `pdftotext -layout`. I counted the scholarly body beginning at **“1 Introduction and research motivation”** and ending at the end of **“8 Conclusions”**. I excluded the abstract/front matter, running headers and page furniture, references, author contributions, funding, declarations, license text, footnote URLs, and the extracted blocks corresponding to Tables 1–10 and Figures 1–4/captions. Hyphenated line-breaks were joined before tokenization; words were counted with a Unicode-aware alphanumeric tokenizer. Because PDF text extraction can interleave table/figure material and equation fragments, the 8,298-word result is an approximate accessible main-text count rather than an exact publisher word total. The PDF page count is 23.
