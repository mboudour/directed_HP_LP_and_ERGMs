Because the accessible source is a two-column, typeset “Article in Press” manuscript PDF, text extraction does not permit perfectly reliable removal of every table, figure, and caption without risking deletion of adjacent body text; the reported value is therefore an approximate body count and may include some extracted table/caption tokens.
