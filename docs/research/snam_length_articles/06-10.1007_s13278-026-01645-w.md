## Count method

I downloaded the official Springer PDF and extracted its text with `pdftotext -layout`. I counted the text range beginning at the first `1 Introduction` heading and ending immediately before `Declarations`, thereby excluding the abstract/front matter, declarations, references, and appendix. I then removed recurring publisher/page headers, isolated page numbers and extraction artifacts, explicit table/figure captions, and lines that were clearly table/matrix or figure-only numeric material. The remaining alphabetic word tokens produced **9,590** words. Because the PDF’s multi-column tables, equations, and figure labels are interleaved with prose in text extraction, this is an approximate body count rather than an exact semantic exclusion; prose surrounding tables/figures is retained. The official page itself exposes the abstract, metadata, declarations, and appendix but not all body sections in the fetched HTML, so the PDF was necessary for the estimate.

**Length note:** Approximate main-text length from Introduction through Conclusions; residual table/equation/figure-adjacent extraction may cause a small over- or under-count. No correction notice was found; the publisher page explicitly identifies the item as an Original Article.
