## Count method and result

The official Springer PDF was downloaded and converted to text with `pdftotext -layout`. I counted word-like tokens from the start of **“1 Introduction”** through the end of **“7 Conclusion”**, stopping before the References section. I excluded the PDF’s running headers/footers, identified figure captions (Figs. 1–6), the Table 1/2 blocks, and all post-conclusion declarations/licensing material. The result is an **approximate body count of 5,917 words**: PDF line wrapping, equations, and publisher typesetting artifacts mean exact browser-style word counting is not feasible from the accessible PDF text alone.

The SpringerLink page identifies the record as an open-access **Original Article**, provides the DOI and publisher PDF, and does not identify it as a correction.
